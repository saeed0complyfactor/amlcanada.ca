Is AML training required in Canada?

Yes, if you have employees, agents or others authorized to act on your behalf. In that case a written, ongoing compliance training program, with a plan for delivering it, is one of the required elements of a compliance program for businesses regulated by FINTRAC. Training should fit your business and your risks, and reach the people who deal with clients or transactions. A sole proprietor with no employees, agents or others authorized to act for them doesn't need a training program.

Training for each role

Front-line staff learn to recognize unusual activity, identify clients and know when and how to escalate. Compliance teams cover reviews, reporting, the 24-hour rule and deadlines, and how to document decisions. Senior management covers their responsibilities for the program and how to oversee it.

Training records

Being able to show who was trained, on what and when is part of showing your program works. We help you keep those records, ready for your effectiveness review or a FINTRAC examination.

Is AML training mandatory in Canada?

Yes, if you have employees, agents or others authorized to act for you. A sole proprietor with no employees, agents or others acting for them doesn't need a training program.

Who needs AML training?

Anyone whose work touches your compliance obligations, typically front-line staff, agents, compliance teams and senior management.

Do you provide training records?

Compliance programs

FINTRAC compliance program requirements

Every business FINTRAC regulates must establish and implement a compliance program. It has five parts: a compliance officer, written policies and procedures, a risk assessment, an ongoing training program and plan, and a review of the program at least every two years.

The first 90 days of AML compliance after FINTRAC registration

Once a money services business in Canada is registered with FINTRAC, its obligations apply from the day it starts operating. This guide sets out a practical order for the first three months and the legal deadlines to put in your diary.

Outsourced or in-house compliance officer?

Every business FINTRAC regulates needs a compliance officer. You can appoint the owner or a manager, hire someone, or outsource the role, and FINTRAC's expectations are the same in each case.

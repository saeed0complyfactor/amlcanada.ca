MSB registration with FINTRAC, prepared properly.

For money services businesses, foreign MSBs and virtual currency dealers. Fixed fee US$3,500.

Book a consultation Read on Fixed fee · US$3,500

Money services businesses (MSBs) must register with FINTRAC before they start operating, and so must foreign businesses that serve people in Canada.

FINTRAC, the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada, registers money services businesses under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA). It's how foreign exchange dealers, money transfer businesses, virtual currency dealers and others become allowed to operate in Canada.

We work out whether and how registration applies to you, gather and check everything FINTRAC asks for, support you through submission and FINTRAC's questions, and put your compliance program in place alongside it.

Our fee is a fixed US$3,500. FINTRAC itself doesn't charge to register.

Who has to register as an MSB

A business with a place of business in Canada must register with FINTRAC before it operates as a money services business. FINTRAC's guidance lists these activities:

  • foreign exchange dealing;
  • remitting or transmitting funds;
  • issuing or redeeming money orders, traveller's cheques or similar negotiable instruments;
  • dealing in virtual currency;
  • crowdfunding platform services;
  • armoured car services;
  • cheque cashing;
  • acquirer services for private automated banking machines.

Registering or being licensed with a province or territory doesn't replace FINTRAC registration. Whether your own activities are covered depends on how your business works, which is the first thing we confirm with you.

Foreign MSB registration

A foreign money services business (FMSB) has no place of business in Canada but directs and provides money services to people or entities in Canada. It must register with FINTRAC before it starts. FINTRAC treats a business as directing services at Canada if, for example, its marketing targets people in Canada, it uses a .ca domain, or it's listed in a Canadian business directory.

A foreign MSB must name a representative for service who lives in Canada. The foreign business itself, not the representative, stays responsible for meeting its obligations.

What FINTRAC asks for

  • details of your business, including incorporation, ownership and senior management;
  • your bank account information;
  • your compliance officer;
  • how many employees you have, and every location you operate from;
  • estimated annual transaction totals for each money service you offer;
  • any agents or mandataries that provide services on your behalf;
  • for a foreign MSB, your representative for service in Canada;
  • documents confirming the business exists and how it's owned and controlled, such as a certificate of incorporation;
  • criminal record checks.

Criminal record checks are needed for the chief executive officer, the president, directors, and anyone who owns or controls 20% or more of the business, directly or indirectly. They must come from a competent authority in the person's country of residence, be dated no more than six months before you submit them, and be translated if they aren't in English or French. Gathering these from several countries is often what takes longest, so we start early.

How registration works with us

  1. Assessment. Which of your services are money services, whether you're an MSB or a foreign MSB, and what follows from that.
  2. Preparation. A checklist of everything FINTRAC needs, and help gathering and checking it.
  3. Submission. Your registration, submitted to FINTRAC with your approval.
  4. Follow-up. Answers to FINTRAC's questions until your registration is decided.
  5. Compliance program. Built alongside your registration, not after it, so you're ready to operate.

After you register

Registration is valid for two years and has to be renewed before it expires. Between renewals, you must tell FINTRAC within 30 days when your registration information changes, such as a new address, new owners or directors, or a change to the services you offer.

Registration is the start, not the finish. A registered MSB also needs a working compliance program, client identification, record keeping, and reporting to FINTRAC. Records generally have to be kept for at least five years.

Virtual currency dealers

Dealing in virtual currency is a money service, so crypto exchanges and dealers register with FINTRAC as MSBs, including foreign ones that serve people in Canada. Once registered, they have additional obligations, such as reporting virtual currency transactions of $10,000 or more within five working days. Provincial securities rules can also apply to some crypto trading platforms; we'll tell you if that's something you need separate advice on.

MSB registration: questions

Still not sure? Book a consultation or write to info@amlcanada.ca.

How much does MSB registration cost?

FINTRAC doesn't charge a registration fee. Our fee to prepare and support your registration is a fixed US$3,500.

How long does FINTRAC registration take?

It depends on how complete your application is, how quickly criminal record checks arrive, and FINTRAC's review and any follow-up questions. Thorough preparation avoids the delays that come from incomplete applications.

Can a company outside Canada register as an MSB?

Yes. A foreign business that directs and provides money services to people in Canada registers as a foreign MSB and must name a representative for service who lives in Canada.

Do I need a compliance program before I register?

You need one to operate. FINTRAC asks for your compliance officer when you register, and your obligations start as soon as you begin. We build your program alongside your registration. Program documents are a separate fixed fee of US$3,500.

How often do I renew?

Every two years. Changes to your registration information have to be reported within 30 days.

Do crypto businesses register with FINTRAC?

Yes. Dealing in virtual currency is a money service, so crypto dealers and exchanges register as MSBs.

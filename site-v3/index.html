    The rules come with numbers.

    You'll meet these five early. We build your program around them.

    1. $10,000

      The reporting threshold

      Receiving $10,000 or more in cash or virtual currency, or sending or receiving an international electronic funds transfer of $10,000 or more, means a report to FINTRAC. Smaller amounts that add up to that within 24 hours count too.

      Covered in compliance programs

    2. 5

      Parts of every compliance program

      A compliance officer, written policies and procedures, a risk assessment, an ongoing training program and an effectiveness review.

      Covered in compliance programs

    3. 2years

      Between effectiveness reviews, at most

      Your program has to be tested at least every two years to show it works as written. MSB registration also has to be renewed every two years.

      Covered in effectiveness reviews

    4. 30days

      To report changes to your registration

      A new address, a new owner or a new service: registered MSBs have to tell FINTRAC within 30 days of the change.

      Covered in MSB registration

    5. 5years

      To keep your records

      Client identification, transaction records and the reports you file generally have to be kept for at least five years.

      Covered in compliance programs

    Questions

    What businesses usually ask us first.

    Who has to register with FINTRAC as a money services business?

    Any business with a place of business in Canada that deals in foreign exchange, remits or transmits funds, issues or redeems money orders or traveller's cheques, deals in virtual currency, provides crowdfunding platform services, provides armoured car services, cashes cheques or provides acquirer services for private automated banking machines. Foreign businesses that direct and provide these services to people in Canada must register too. More on MSB registration.

    Does registration have to be renewed?

    Yes. MSB registration is renewed every two years, and changes to your registration information have to be reported within 30 days.

    Do foreign MSBs need to register with FINTRAC?

    Yes. A business with no place of business in Canada that directs and provides money services to people in Canada must register before it starts, and must name a representative for service who lives in Canada. We support foreign businesses with registration, a Canadian business address and an outsourced compliance officer.

    Do payment service providers register with the Bank of Canada?

    Yes. Payment service providers that perform retail payment activities for end users in Canada must register with the Bank of Canada under the Retail Payment Activities Act (RPAA). More on RPAA registration.

    What does a compliance program include?

    Five parts: a compliance officer, written policies and procedures, a risk assessment, an ongoing training program and an effectiveness review at least every two years. Our fixed fee of US$3,500 covers 14 program documents.

    Do we need our own compliance officer?

    Your business has to appoint one. We can act as your outsourced compliance officer at US$1,500 per 10 hours, or support the person you appoint.

    Our bank has asked for our AML program. What now?

    Book a consultation. We'll look at what you have, tell you what's missing and agree a written scope to close the gaps.

    What is an effectiveness review?

    A review of your compliance program, at least every two years, that tests whether your policies, risk assessment and training work in practice, not just on paper.

    FINTRAC has contacted us about an examination. Where do we start?

    Start with a consultation. We review your program and records with you and help you prepare to show how it works.

    How does an engagement start?

    With a consultation about your business and what FINTRAC expects of it. Then we send a written scope covering the work, who does which part and a timeline, agreed before anything starts.

    What do your services cost?

    Most have a fixed fee: Canadian incorporation US$1,500, MSB registration US$3,500, compliance program documents US$3,500, RPAA registration US$3,500 plus the Bank of Canada's fee, and an outsourced compliance officer at US$1,500 per 10 hours. See all fees.

    Can we start with one service?

    Yes. Most businesses start with the gap that's most urgent and add the rest when they need it.

How to register an MSB in Canada

Guide · Updated 25 October 2026 · 3 min read · AML Canada

Reviewed by Muhammad Saeed Abbasi, CAMS, ACMA, CGMA, MLRO and Managing Director, ComplyFactor Limited. Last checked 25 October 2026.

A money services business must register with FINTRAC before it starts operating, and renew every two years. FINTRAC doesn't charge a fee, and it says the majority of complete applications are processed within three months.

Key points

  • Register with FINTRAC before you start operating. FINTRAC doesn't charge a fee.
  • Criminal record checks are needed for the CEO, president, directors and 20%+ owners.
  • Answer FINTRAC's clarification requests within 30 days.
  • Registration lasts two years. Report changes within 30 days.

Step 1: Check whether you need to register

In Canada, a money services business (MSB) is a business that provides certain money services. If you have a place of business in Canada and you provide any of these, you must register with FINTRAC, the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada, before you start:

  • foreign exchange dealing;
  • remitting or transmitting funds;
  • issuing or redeeming money orders, traveller's cheques or similar negotiable instruments;
  • dealing in virtual currency;
  • crowdfunding platform services;
  • armoured car services;
  • cheque cashing;
  • acquirer services for private automated banking machines.

If you have no place of business in Canada but you direct and provide these services to people or entities in Canada, you're a foreign MSB, and you must register too. Our foreign MSB guide covers that case.

Registering or being licensed with a province doesn't replace FINTRAC registration. Quebec has its own licence for money-services businesses, issued by Revenu Québec, which is needed in addition to FINTRAC registration.

If your business holds funds for users or transfers funds electronically for them, check the Retail Payment Activities Act as well. Many payment businesses need to register with the Bank of Canada as payment service providers, separately from FINTRAC. Our RPAA guide explains who is covered.

Step 2: Gather what FINTRAC asks for

FINTRAC's registration asks for:

  • details of the business, including its incorporation, ownership and senior management;
  • bank account information;
  • your compliance officer;
  • how many employees you have, and every location you operate from;
  • estimated annual transaction totals for each money service you offer;
  • any agents or mandataries that provide services on your behalf;
  • for a foreign MSB, a representative for service who lives in Canada;
  • documents confirming the business exists and how it's owned and controlled;
  • criminal record checks.

If you use agents or mandataries, you must also check that they're eligible before you engage them, including reviewing their criminal record checks. This has applied since October 1, 2025. For agents engaged before that date, MSBs have until October 1, 2027, according to FINTRAC.

Step 3: Order criminal record checks early

FINTRAC needs criminal record checks for the chief executive officer, the president, the directors, and anyone who owns or controls 20% or more of the business, directly or indirectly. A sole proprietor provides one for themselves.

Each check must come from a competent authority in the person's country of residence and be issued no more than six months before you apply. If a check isn't in English or French, it needs a translation attested by a certified translator, as FINTRAC's registration page sets out. When owners and directors live in different countries, this is often the step that takes longest, so start it first.

Step 4: Submit and answer FINTRAC's questions

Once everything is gathered and checked, you submit your registration to FINTRAC. FINTRAC may send clarification requests before it decides. You have 30 days to answer each one, and if you don't, FINTRAC may deny the application (PCMLTFA section 11.14). Once you're registered, the same 30-day rule applies, and FINTRAC may revoke a registration if a request goes unanswered.

Complete, consistent information is the best way to avoid back-and-forth. The transaction estimates, locations, ownership and documents should all tell the same story.

FINTRAC says the majority of complete applications are processed within three months, and more complex ones can take longer. In our experience it has ranged from about a week to seven months, depending mostly on how complete the application is and on FINTRAC's workload. Our MSB registration service covers preparing and submitting the application.

Step 5: Have your compliance program ready

Registration allows you to operate. Your obligations under the law start at the same time. From the day you start, you need a compliance program with five elements: a compliance officer, written policies and procedures, a risk assessment, an ongoing training program, and an effectiveness review at least every two years. You also need to identify clients, keep records, generally for at least five years, and report to FINTRAC.

It helps to build the program while the registration is in progress, so it's in place when you're registered. Our compliance program documents are written for this.

Step 6: Stay registered

MSB registration is valid for two years and must be renewed before it expires. Between renewals, tell FINTRAC within 30 days when your registration information changes: a new address, new owners or directors, a new compliance officer, or a change in the services you offer. FINTRAC explains how on its update and renewal page.

How to register an MSB: common questions

Still not sure? Book a consultation or write to info@amlcanada.ca.

Does FINTRAC charge to register an MSB?

No. FINTRAC doesn't charge a registration fee. Your costs are your own time, criminal record checks, any certified translations, and professional help if you use it.

Is there an MSB licence in Canada?

At the federal level, Canada requires registration with FINTRAC under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act. When people search for an MSB licence in Canada, they usually mean FINTRAC registration. Quebec is the exception: Revenu Québec issues a separate licence for money-services businesses.

Can I register an MSB without a Canadian office?

Yes, as a foreign MSB, if you direct and provide money services to people in Canada. You'll need a representative for service who lives in Canada.

Do I need a compliance program before registering?

You need it in place to operate, and FINTRAC asks for your compliance officer when you register. It makes sense to build both together.

How long is MSB registration valid?

Two years. Changes to your registration information must be reported within 30 days.

How long does FINTRAC take to approve an MSB registration?

FINTRAC says the majority of complete applications are processed within three months. In our experience it has ranged from about a week to seven months.

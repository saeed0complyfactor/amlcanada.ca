We keep engagements simple: a conversation, a written scope, the work itself, and support afterwards.

You agree the scope and the fee before any work begins.

Four steps, in the same order every time.

You always know which step you're in and what comes next.

We start with a conversation.

About your business, your timeline and what's required of you. You leave it with a clear next step.

  • Your business model and the services you offer
  • Your deadlines, such as a launch or an examination
  • What you already have in place

We write down what we'll do.

What we'll do, what we need from you, and when. Nothing starts until you've agreed it.

  • The work, in plain language
  • What we need from you, and when
  • Timeline and milestones

We do the work with you.

We explain each decision as we go, so your team can stand behind it.

  • Regular check-ins
  • Drafts you can comment on
  • The reasons behind each decision, in writing

We stay for what comes next.

Your obligations change as your business does: the next product, the next market or the next review.

  • Updates when your business changes
  • Help with questions from FINTRAC
  • Your next effectiveness review

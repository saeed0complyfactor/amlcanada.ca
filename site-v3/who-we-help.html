Six kinds of business we work with, and the services that usually help them first.

  1. Money services businesses

    Foreign exchange dealers, money transfer businesses and others that need to register with FINTRAC, stay compliant and report on time.

    Services that usually help
    Buy or register an MSBMSB registrationCompliance programsOutsourced compliance officerEffectiveness reviews

  2. Virtual currency dealers

    Crypto exchanges and dealers in virtual currency, which register with FINTRAC as money services businesses and report large virtual currency transactions.

    Services that usually help
    MSB registrationCompliance programsOutsourced compliance officer

  3. Payment service providers

    Payment processors, wallets and other PSPs that register with the Bank of Canada under the RPAA, and often with FINTRAC too.

    Services that usually help
    RPAA registrationRPAA compliance programMSB registration

  4. Foreign businesses entering Canada

    Companies outside Canada that serve customers in Canada and need a Canadian company, registration, a Canadian address, a compliance officer, or a registered company to acquire.

    Services that usually help
    Buy or register an MSBCompany incorporationCanadian addressMSB registrationMSB for saleOutsourced compliance officerApostille & notarization

  5. Payment and fintech companies

    Start-ups and growing companies that need to understand their obligations and meet them as they scale.

    Services that usually help
    Compliance programsRPAA registrationTraining

  6. Compliance officers

    Compliance officers at reporting entities who need a second pair of hands, a review, or training for their teams.

    Services that usually help
    Outsourced compliance officerEffectiveness reviewsTraining

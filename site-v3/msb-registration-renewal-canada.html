FINTRAC MSB registration renewal: how and when to renew

Guide · Published 11 November 2026 · 6 min read · AML Canada

FINTRAC MSB registration renewal is due every two years under section 11.19 of the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act, and FINTRAC says you must renew before the expiry date. A renewal filed in time keeps you Registered while FINTRAC processes it. This guide covers the dates, the information to refresh and the 30-day deadlines that run alongside renewal.

Key points

  • Registration lasts two years, and the renewal must reach FINTRAC before the expiry date.
  • Virtual currency dealers and foreign MSBs have a set date for their first renewal under section 6.1 of the Registration Regulations.
  • A renewal carries the same Schedule 1 information as an application, so refresh owners, accounts, agents and volumes first.
  • Changes still go to FINTRAC within 30 days, whether or not a renewal is due.

How do I renew my FINTRAC registration?

You renew by completing FINTRAC's renewal form, "Request to renew the registration of a money services business or foreign money services business", before your registration expires. FINTRAC's renewal page says registration is valid for two years and that it's your responsibility to renew on time.

The legal steps are short:

  1. File the renewal. Section 11.19 of the Act requires a registered business to renew "in the prescribed form and manner every two years". Under section 4 of the Registration Regulations, you submit electronically if you have the technical capability, or by telephone if you don't.
  2. Give the full Schedule 1 information. Section 5 of the Regulations says a renewal application must contain the information in Schedule 1, the same schedule used for a first application. Section 6.01 adds that you must report all known information that falls within each item.
  3. Answer any clarification request. FINTRAC says it will send one if it needs more information. A registered business has 30 days after the request to respond (Act, s. 11.17(1)).

As of 11 November 2026, FINTRAC says the majority of complete applications are processed within three months of receiving a complete package, and complex ones can take longer.

When is FINTRAC MSB registration renewal due?

For most MSBs, renewal is due two years after registration. Section 11.19 of the Act sets the two-year cycle, and FINTRAC's registry describes an Expired business as one that "did not renew its registration after 2 years".

Two groups have a fixed deadline for their first renewal under section 6.1 of the Registration Regulations: virtual currency dealers (paragraph 5(h)(iv) of the Act) and foreign money services businesses (paragraph 5(h.1)). They must renew no later than:

  • A person: the last day of the month of their first birthday that occurs after the second anniversary of initial registration.
  • A corporation: the last day of the month of the first anniversary of its incorporation that occurs after the second anniversary of initial registration.
  • Another entity: the day after the second anniversary of initial registration.

Your expiry date is public. Section 2 of the Regulations makes the date a registration expires part of the identifying information shown on the registry. Look up your own listing and put that date in your compliance calendar.

What to check and update before you renew

Because a renewal must carry the full Schedule 1 information, review each item against how the business runs today. A renewal that repeats the old application can leave the registry inaccurate. These are the main Schedule 1 items to confirm:

CheckSchedule 1 itemWhat to confirm
Identity and servicesPart A, items 1, 5 to 9Legal and operating name, place of business, phone, website, every service you're renewing for, and your existing registration number
People and ownersPart B, item 7 or 8CEO, president, each director and everyone owning or controlling 20% or more, with contact details, date of birth and country of birth and residence
Representative for servicePart B, item 8.1For a foreign MSB, the phone number and email of the person in Canada authorized to accept FINTRAC notices
Bank accountsPart B, item 9Every financial entity, in Canada or abroad, where you hold an account used for MSB services
Other MSBs you usePart B, item 10Name, address and FINTRAC registration number of each MSB or foreign MSB you use to conduct transactions

Check eligibility too. Under section 11.11 of the Act and section 3 of the Regulations, a corporation isn't eligible if its CEO, president, a director or a 20% owner falls into certain groups, such as people convicted of listed offences. FINTRAC's harm done guide for registration violations says registration information that is incomplete, outdated or unclear diminishes the effectiveness of its compliance activities.

Renewal deadlines and the 30-day rules

Renewal is one of several registration deadlines, and the others keep running between renewals. Each comes from the Act or the Regulations:

EventDeadlineSource
Routine renewalEvery two years, before the expiry dateAct s. 11.19; FINTRAC
First renewal, virtual currency dealer or foreign MSBThe date set by the person, corporation or entity ruleRegulations s. 6.1
Change to registration information, or newly obtained informationWithin 30 days after you become aware of itAct s. 11.13(1)
Foreign MSB's representative for service changes name or addressWithin 30 days, or FINTRAC must deny or revokeAct s. 11.13(2)
Clarification request from FINTRACWithin 30 days after the request is madeAct s. 11.17(1)
You stop a registered activityWithin 30 days after you cease itAct s. 11.2

For planning, work back from the expiry date. If FINTRAC's three-month processing estimate applies to your file, starting the review several months ahead leaves time to gather owner details and answer questions. A renewal doesn't replace a change notice. If a director changes six weeks before your renewal is due, the 30-day rule in section 11.13(1) still applies. Our guide to reporting a change of ownership covers that process.

Can you keep operating while the renewal is processed?

Yes, if you submitted the renewal before the expiry date. FINTRAC's registry says a business that has submitted a renewal application but has passed its expiry date "remains registered with FINTRAC until its renewal is processed" and shows as Registered.

Watch for email during this period. FINTRAC says clarification requests usually go by email to the contact person or representative for service on file, and delays in responding can increase processing times. If a registered business doesn't answer within 30 days, FINTRAC may revoke the registration (Act, s. 11.17(2)).

What happens if my MSB registration expires?

If no renewal reached FINTRAC before the expiry date, the registry says the business "is deemed to have an expired registration" and its status becomes Expired. The business is then no longer registered, and section 11.1 of the Act requires every MSB and foreign MSB to be registered with FINTRAC.

FINTRAC's harm done guide says that when there's a significant delay in renewing, it "may consider that the MSB is operating as an unregistered MSB". The consequences can be administrative or criminal:

  • Penalty. Failing to renew under section 11.19 is a serious violation in the schedule to the Administrative Monetary Penalties Regulations. As of 11 November 2026, section 5(b) of those Regulations sets the range for a serious violation at $1 to $4,000,000.
  • Offence. Under section 77.4 of the Act, a business that must register and knowingly engages in an activity it isn't registered for commits an offence. The maximum is a $2,500,000 fine, two years less a day in prison, or both, on summary conviction, and a $5,000,000 fine, five years in prison, or both, on indictment.

FINTRAC's renewal page lists the same risk: failure to renew may result in administrative monetary penalties or criminal charges. Our FINTRAC penalties guide explains how penalties are set. Denials, revocations and reapplying are covered in our guide to denied, revoked and expired registrations.

If you don't plan to renew

If the business no longer offers money services, notify FINTRAC instead of letting the registration lapse. Section 11.2 of the Act requires a registered business that ceases an activity to notify FINTRAC within 30 days after it stops. FINTRAC uses a cessation form for this, and the notice carries the proposed effective date of cessation (Regulations, Schedule 2).

The registry then shows the business as Ceased, which FINTRAC defines as a business that notified it that it no longer offers the services. If you plan to start again later, our guide on how to register an MSB sets out the full application.

Registration renewal: common questions

Still not sure? Book a consultation or write to info@amlcanada.ca.

How do I renew my FINTRAC registration?

Complete FINTRAC's renewal form before your registration expires, with the full information in Schedule 1 of the Registration Regulations. Answer any clarification request within 30 days. FINTRAC says most complete applications are processed within three months.

What happens if my MSB registration expires?

If you didn't submit a renewal before the expiry date, FINTRAC's registry treats the registration as expired. Failing to renew is a serious violation under the penalty regulations, and knowingly providing money services while unregistered can be prosecuted under section 77.4 of the Act.

How often do I renew MSB registration with FINTRAC?

Every two years, under section 11.19 of the Act. Virtual currency dealers and foreign MSBs follow the date rules in section 6.1 of the Registration Regulations for their first renewal.

Do I still report changes if my renewal is coming up?

Yes. Section 11.13(1) of the Act requires you to report changes to your registration information within 30 days after you become aware of them, separately from renewal.

Is MSB renewal a licence renewal?

No. People often search for an MSB licence, but FINTRAC says registration doesn't mean it endorses or licenses a business, and it doesn't issue licences or certificates of registration. What you renew is your registration.

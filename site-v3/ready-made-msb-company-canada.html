Ready-made FINTRAC MSB companies: what you get and what to question

Guide · Updated 25 October 2026 · 2 min read · AML Canada

A ready-made MSB is a Canadian company that's already registered with FINTRAC, sold through a share purchase. Here's what should come with one, and which sales claims don't hold up.

Key points

  • You buy the company's shares. The registration stays with the company.
  • Ask for the legal name and MSB registration number before any deposit.
  • Changes such as new owners and directors go to FINTRAC within 30 days.
  • A registration doesn't come with a bank account.

On this page

  1. What "ready-made" means
  2. What should come with it
  3. Claims to question
  4. What drives the price
  5. Before you pay a deposit

What "ready-made" means

A ready-made MSB is a Canadian corporation, usually newly incorporated and with no trading history, that a seller has already registered with FINTRAC as a money services business. You acquire it by buying all its shares. The FINTRAC registration belongs to the registered business, so it stays with the company; it can't be moved to a company of yours.

The appeal is starting from a company already on the FINTRAC registry instead of waiting for a new application. The trade-off is that you take on whatever comes with the company, so the checks come before the price.

What should come with it

  • the certificate and articles of incorporation, the minute book and the share register;
  • FINTRAC's registration approval and the company's listing on the public registry (new registrations can take up to a month to appear);
  • the share purchase agreement and the share transfer documents;
  • the change filings to FINTRAC after closing;
  • any compliance documents, reviewed and rewritten for your business. A program written for nobody in particular won't fit yours.

Claims to question

You may hearWhat to know
"Licensed by FINTRAC"FINTRAC registers MSBs and says it doesn't issue licences or endorse businesses. See MSB licence in Canada
"Start trading the same day"The new owners, directors and compliance officer are reported within 30 days, and the business needs a compliance program, a compliance officer and banking before it operates
"Bank account included"Registration doesn't guarantee banking. An existing account may not survive a change of control. Ask the bank, not the seller
"The licence transfers to your company"It doesn't. The registration stays with the company whose shares you buy
"PSP included"Bank of Canada registration under the RPAA is separate from FINTRAC registration. If the company has it, it must be re-registered before the purchase closes

What drives the price

The registered services, how long is left before the registration expires, whether the company has traded and how clean its records are, whether it's also registered with the Bank of Canada, and what's included after closing. Our registered companies start from €28,000. Registering a new MSB instead is our fixed US$3,500, with no FINTRAC fee. See buy or register.

Before you pay a deposit

  1. Get the legal name and MSB registration number, under an NDA if the seller asks.
  2. Check the status, services and expiry date on FINTRAC's registry.
  3. Confirm that the new owners, directors, CEO and president can provide criminal record checks and aren't ineligible under PCMLTFA s.11.11.
  4. Ask whether the company is registered with the Bank of Canada, and plan any re-registration.
  5. Read the share purchase agreement for warranties about the company's history.

Ready-made MSB company: common questions

Still not sure? Book a consultation or write to info@amlcanada.ca.

What is a ready-made MSB in Canada?

A Canadian company already registered with FINTRAC as a money services business, sold by transferring its shares. The registration stays with the company.

How much does a ready-made MSB cost?

Our registered companies start from €28,000. The price depends on the registered services, the time left on the registration, the company's history and any Bank of Canada registration.

Does a ready-made MSB come with a bank account?

Not reliably. FINTRAC registration doesn't guarantee banking, and an existing account may not continue after a change of control.

Can I start operating right after the purchase?

Only once you have a compliance program and compliance officer that fit the new owners, and banking in place. The changes are reported to FINTRAC within 30 days.

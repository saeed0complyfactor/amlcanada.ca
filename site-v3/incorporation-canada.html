Federal or provincial?

You can incorporate federally, under the Canada Business Corporations Act (CBCA) through Corporations Canada, or in a province or territory, such as Ontario under its Business Corporations Act. A federal company can use its name across Canada, though it still registers in the provinces where it does business. A provincial company is often simpler if you'll operate mainly in one province. We help you choose before anything is filed.

Directors and residency

Federal corporations must have at least 25% resident Canadian directors; if the company has fewer than four directors, at least one must be a resident Canadian. A resident Canadian includes a Canadian citizen ordinarily resident in Canada and a permanent resident. Ontario doesn't require resident Canadian directors, which can matter for founders based outside Canada. Some regulated sectors have stricter rules.

How long it takes

Once we have your details and documents, incorporation usually takes a few hours to 24 hours. Corporations Canada processes online incorporations in about a day, with a 4-hour express option for an extra government fee, and Ontario incorporates immediately online.

What it costs

Our fee is a fixed US$1,500. Government fees are paid separately to the government: currently CA$200 for federal online incorporation (CA$100 more for 4-hour express) or CA$300 for Ontario online incorporation. If you choose a named company rather than a numbered one, a name search report is usually needed too.

After incorporation

  • Keep corporate records, including a register of individuals with significant control.
  • File an annual return each year (federal online annual return: CA$12).
  • Register for tax accounts, and in each province where you carry on business.
  • Register with FINTRAC as an MSB or with the Bank of Canada under the RPAA before you start offering those services.

How much does it cost to incorporate a company in Canada?

Our fee is a fixed US$1,500. Government filing fees are separate: currently CA$200 federally online or CA$300 in Ontario online.

How long does incorporation take?

Usually a few hours to 24 hours once we have your details and documents.

Can a non-resident incorporate in Canada?

Yes. Federal companies need at least 25% resident Canadian directors (at least one if there are fewer than four). Ontario doesn't require resident Canadian directors.

Should we incorporate before registering as an MSB?

Usually, yes. The company is what registers with FINTRAC, so incorporating first means the registration can start straight away.

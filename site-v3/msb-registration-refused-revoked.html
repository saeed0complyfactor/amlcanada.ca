MSB registration denied, revoked or expired: what happens and what to do

Guide · Published 21 October 2026 · 5 min read · AML Canada

FINTRAC can deny an MSB application or revoke a registration for a short list of reasons set out in the Act. A registration that isn't renewed expires. Each outcome has its own deadlines, and operating without a valid registration is an offence.

Key points

  • Ineligibility revokes a registration automatically, from the day it arises.
  • Unanswered 30-day clarification requests can lead to denial or revocation.
  • You have 30 days to ask FINTRAC's Director to review a denial or revocation.
  • Renew before expiry: a renewal submitted in time keeps you Registered while it's processed.

The four statuses on FINTRAC's registry

FINTRAC's MSB registry shows one of four statuses for every business:

StatusFINTRAC's meaning
RegisteredCurrently registered with FINTRAC
RevokedNo longer registered for not answering a clarification request, not providing assistance to FINTRAC, or being found ineligible
ExpiredDidn't renew its registration after two years
CeasedTold FINTRAC it no longer offers the services

FINTRAC updates the registry monthly, and a downloadable version is available. It also keeps a separate list of businesses with revoked registrations. Search by name and registration number, and check the expiry date as well as the status.

Why an application is denied

The Act gives three grounds:

  • Ineligibility. FINTRAC must deny an applicant listed in subsection 11.11(1) (section 11.16).
  • Unanswered clarification requests. FINTRAC may deny an application if the applicant doesn't provide requested clarifications within 30 days (section 11.14).
  • A foreign MSB's representative for service. If the representative's name or address for service changes and FINTRAC isn't told within 30 days, FINTRAC must deny the application (subsection 11.13(2)).

The ineligible group includes people and entities subject to certain sanctions, listed terrorist entities, and those convicted of money laundering, terrorist financing and a range of other offences, including human smuggling and trafficking offences under sections 117 and 118 of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act. Since the Bill C-12 amendments of March 26, 2026, it also includes anyone who still hasn't paid a FINTRAC penalty, with interest, 30 days after all proceedings on the violation have ended. Under the Registration Regulations, a corporation or other entity is also ineligible if its CEO, president, a director or a 20% owner falls into most of these groups. FINTRAC's eligibility page lists the offences in plain language.

Why a registration is revoked

  • Ineligibility. If a registered business becomes ineligible, its registration is revoked as of the day that happens (subsection 11.11(2)). FINTRAC then sends written notice without delay. No decision by FINTRAC is needed for the revocation to take effect.
  • Clarification requests. A registered business must answer FINTRAC's clarification requests within 30 days, or FINTRAC may revoke (section 11.17).
  • Not assisting FINTRAC. FINTRAC may revoke if a business doesn't give an examiner reasonable assistance, or doesn't provide documents required by a notice served during an examination (section 11.171).
  • A foreign MSB's representative for service. The same 30-day rule as for applications applies, and revocation is mandatory.

Every decision to deny or revoke must be in writing, with reasons (section 11.18).

Asking for a review, then appealing

Within 30 days after you receive a denial or revocation, you can apply in writing to FINTRAC's Director for a review and include supporting information (section 11.3). The Director either confirms the decision or substitutes their own, and serves notice with reasons.

If you disagree with the Director's decision, you can appeal to the Federal Court within 30 days after it's served, or within a longer period the Court allows. If the Director doesn't decide within 90 days after receiving your application for review, you can appeal within 30 days after that 90-day period ends (section 11.4).

FINTRAC's reviews and appeals page says applications go to its Review and Appeals Unit by mail, or electronically after you email rev-app@fintrac-canafe.gc.ca for a secure link. If a lawyer applies for you, include a letter of authorization. This is general information, and a lawyer can advise on your own case.

Expiry and renewal

Registration must be renewed every two years (section 11.19). FINTRAC says it's your responsibility to renew on time, and that most complete applications are processed within three months.

Timing matters because of how the registry treats renewals. If you submit a renewal before the expiry date, you stay Registered until FINTRAC processes it, even if the expiry date passes. If you don't submit one before the expiry date, your registration is treated as Expired.

Virtual currency dealers and foreign MSBs have a specific deadline for their first renewal under section 6.1 of the Registration Regulations. For a corporation, it's the last day of the month of the first anniversary of incorporation that falls after the second anniversary of initial registration. Check the expiry date on your registry listing rather than counting from memory.

Changes and stopping services

Tell FINTRAC about any change to your registration information, or newly obtained information that should have been in the application, within 30 days of becoming aware of it (subsection 11.13(1)). If you stop offering the services you're registered for, notify FINTRAC within 30 days using its cessation form (section 11.2). FINTRAC says failing to do either may lead to penalties or criminal charges.

Operating without a valid registration

Section 77.4 of the Act makes it an offence for a business that must register to knowingly engage in an activity it isn't registered for. As the Act reads today, the maximum is a fine of $2,500,000, up to two years less a day in prison, or both, on summary conviction, and a fine of $5,000,000, up to five years in prison, or both, on indictment. Officers and directors who directed, authorized or took part in an offence can also be found guilty (section 78).

The same failures can be handled as administrative violations instead. Under the penalty regulations, failing to register, to report changes, to answer clarification requests, to renew or to notify cessation are all serious violations. For violations on or after March 26, 2026, a serious violation can carry up to $4,000,000. FINTRAC can't pursue both a penalty and a prosecution for the same act. Our guide to FINTRAC penalties explains the process.

There are practical effects too. FINTRAC notes that financial entities must make sure the foreign MSBs they open accounts for are registered with FINTRAC.

Applying again

The registration sections of the Act don't set a waiting period before a new application. Any ineligibility has to be resolved first, such as an unpaid penalty. The application asks whether you've applied before, so be open about the earlier outcome and what has changed.

Fresh criminal record checks will be needed, issued no more than six months before you apply. Answer every clarification request well within 30 days. Denied, revoked or expired: common questions

Still not sure? Book a consultation or write to info@amlcanada.ca.

What happens if my MSB registration expires?

If you didn't submit a renewal before the expiry date, the registry shows the business as Expired and it isn't registered. Providing money services while unregistered can lead to a penalty or prosecution.

Can I keep operating while my renewal is processed?

Yes, if you submitted the renewal before the expiry date. FINTRAC says the business stays Registered until the renewal is processed.

How do I challenge a FINTRAC revocation?

Apply in writing to FINTRAC's Director for a review within 30 days of receiving the decision. If you disagree with the Director's decision, you can appeal to the Federal Court within 30 days after it's served.

Can a business with a revoked registration apply again?

The Act doesn't set a waiting period, but the reason for revocation must be resolved first. An ineligible applicant must be refused.

How do I check if an MSB is registered?

Search FINTRAC's Money Services Business Registry by name or registration number and check the status and expiry date. FINTRAC says registration isn't an endorsement or a licence.

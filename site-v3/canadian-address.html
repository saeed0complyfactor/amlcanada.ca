Why a Canadian address helps

Canadian banks, payment partners and customers often look for a Canadian presence, and a Canadian business address gives your setup a stable base. For a Canadian-based MSB, FINTRAC registration asks for every location you operate from.

How the address fits your FINTRAC registration

A foreign MSB registers with FINTRAC through a representative for service: a person who resides in Canada and accepts FINTRAC's notices on the business's behalf. That's a separate requirement from your business address.

If you're a foreign MSB, a physical location in Canada can change your status to a domestic MSB. We'll confirm how the address fits your registration before you use it.

Can I use the address on my FINTRAC registration?

It depends on how you're registered. A foreign MSB registers with a representative for service who resides in Canada, and a physical location in Canada can change a foreign MSB into a domestic MSB. We confirm how the address fits your registration before you use it.

Is the address my representative for service?

No. A foreign MSB's representative for service is a person who resides in Canada, which is a separate requirement. Ask us about it in your consultation.

What happens to mail sent to the address?

How mail is handled is set out in your written scope.

Can you use a virtual office for FINTRAC MSB registration?

FINTRAC's published guidance doesn't say whether a virtual office is acceptable. It does say what counts as a place of business in Canada, what the registration asks about your addresses, and that FINTRAC can check the information you give it.

Foreign MSB registration in Canada

What each service costs

Fees are in US dollars unless shown otherwise. Government fees are separate and paid directly to the regulator.

ServiceFeeBasisWhat it coversTypical timing
Outsourced compliance officer US$1,500 per 10 hours · scalable A Canadian AML compliance officer (MLRO): onboarding reviews, FINTRAC reporting, policies and risk assessments. Scoped to your business
MSB registration US$3,500 fixed fee · one time FINTRAC registration for money services businesses, foreign MSBs and virtual currency dealers. 1 week to 7 months
Buy or register an MSB US$3,500 new registration · fixed fee Two routes to a FINTRAC-registered MSB: buy a ready-made company or register your own. 1 week to 7 months (new registration)
Compliance programs US$3,500 fixed fee · program documents 14 PCMLTFA compliance program documents, based on your business. Scoped to your business
RPAA registration US$3,500 fixed fee · plus the Bank of Canada application fee Payment service provider registration with the Bank of Canada under the RPAA. Often 2 to 8 months (not guaranteed)
RPAA compliance program US$3,500 fixed fee · program documents Governance, risk, incident response, safeguarding and continuity frameworks for PSPs. Scoped to your business
MSB for sale €28,000 starting price · acquisition A Canadian company already registered with FINTRAC as a money services business. Scoped to your business
Company incorporation US$1,500 fixed fee · plus government filing fees A Canadian company, federal or Ontario, usually incorporated within a few hours to 24 hours. Usually within 24 hours
Apostille & notarization Quote per document set · plus government fees Notarization and apostilles for Canadian corporate documents used abroad, through a licensed Ontario notary partner. Scoped to your business
Canadian address US$200 per month A Canadian business address for foreign MSBs and companies entering Canada. Scoped to your business
Effectiveness reviews Scoped quoted after your consultation The two-year review FINTRAC requires, and preparation for an examination. Scoped to your business
Training Scoped quoted after your consultation AML training for front-line staff, compliance teams and management. Scoped to your business

Timings are general guides from our experience, not guarantees. Registration decisions are made by FINTRAC and the Bank of Canada and depend on how complete an application is and on their workload.

  • FINTRAC doesn't charge a fee to register a money services business.
  • Incorporation government fees are separate: currently CA$200 federally online, or CA$300 in Ontario online.
  • The Bank of Canada charges RPAA applicants a one-time, non-refundable application fee, paid directly to the Bank. It was CA$2,500 when introduced in November 2024 and is indexed to inflation each January, so we confirm the current amount before you apply. The Bank will also charge registered PSPs annual assessment fees once its cost-recovery regulations are final.
  • Every engagement starts with a consultation and a written scope, so you know the fee before any work begins.

Pricing questions

Still not sure? Book a consultation or write to info@amlcanada.ca.

How much does FINTRAC MSB registration cost?

FINTRAC charges no registration fee. Our fee to prepare and support your registration is a fixed US$3,500.

How much does an AML compliance program cost?

A fixed US$3,500 for 14 PCMLTFA compliance program documents.

How much does an outsourced compliance officer cost?

US$1,500 per block of 10 hours, with hours that scale as your business grows.

How much does RPAA registration cost?

Our fee is a fixed US$3,500, plus the Bank of Canada's one-time application fee (CA$2,500 when introduced, indexed to inflation each year), paid to the Bank. The RPAA compliance program is a further US$3,500.

How much does a registered MSB cost to buy?

Acquisitions start from €28,000, depending on the company.

How much does it cost to incorporate a company in Canada?

Our fee is a fixed US$1,500, usually done within a few hours to 24 hours. Government filing fees are separate.

How long do registrations take?

FINTRAC says most complete MSB applications are processed within three months. In our experience, it has taken about 1 week to 7 months, and Bank of Canada RPAA registration about 2 to 8 months. Incorporation usually takes a few hours to 24 hours.

Why are fees in US dollars?

Our fees are quoted in US dollars, except MSB acquisitions, which are in euros. The Bank of Canada's own fee is in Canadian dollars.

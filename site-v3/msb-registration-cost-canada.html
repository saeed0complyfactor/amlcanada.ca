How much does MSB registration cost in Canada?

Guide · Updated 11 October 2026 · 2 min read · AML Canada

FINTRAC doesn't charge a fee to register a money services business. The costs come from criminal record checks, translations, documents and the compliance program you need to operate.

Key points

  • FINTRAC registration itself is free.
  • Budget for criminal record checks, certified translations and documents.
  • You need a compliance program in place before you operate.
  • Our fixed fees: US$3,500 registration; US$3,500 program documents.

On this page

  1. FINTRAC's fee: none
  2. The other costs of registering
  3. What it costs to operate once registered
  4. What professional help costs with us
  5. Registering vs buying a registered MSB

FINTRAC's fee: none

FINTRAC doesn't charge a fee to register a money services business, or to renew. If anyone tells you there's a government fee for FINTRAC MSB registration, ask what it's for.

The other costs of registering

Criminal record checks are one of the main outside costs. FINTRAC needs one for the CEO, the president, each director and anyone owning or controlling 20% or more, and each must be issued within six months before you apply. Costs vary by country and by provider.

Any check or document that isn't in English or French needs a translation attested by a certified translator. You'll also need corporate documents showing the business exists and how it's owned and controlled.

If you don't have a Canadian company yet, there are government incorporation fees, currently CA$200 federally online or CA$300 in Ontario online. Where provincial licensing applies, there are fees for that too: Quebec licenses money-services businesses separately through Revenu Québec, which sets its own fees.

Then there's time: gathering and checking everything, and answering FINTRAC's clarification requests within 30 days. Our step-by-step registration guide shows where the time goes.

What it costs to operate once registered

To operate, an MSB needs a compliance program: a compliance officer, policies and procedures, a risk assessment, training and an effectiveness review at least every two years. It also has to report to FINTRAC and keep records. These are ongoing costs, whether you staff them in-house or outsource them. Our compliance program documents cover the written part at a fixed fee.

Registering vs buying a registered MSB

Some businesses buy a company that's already registered instead of registering a new one. It costs more up front. It still needs due diligence, updates to FINTRAC within 30 days of changes like new owners and directors, criminal record checks for the new people, and a compliance program that fits the new owners. Is FINTRAC MSB registration free?

Yes. FINTRAC doesn't charge to register or renew.

How much does it cost to set up an MSB in Canada?

Registration is free, but budget for criminal record checks, certified translations, documents and a compliance program. Our fixed fees are US$3,500 for registration and US$3,500 for compliance program documents.

Does Quebec charge for an MSB licence?

Yes. Quebec licenses money-services businesses separately through Revenu Québec, which sets its own fees.

How to register an MSB in Canada

A money services business must register with FINTRAC before it starts operating, and renew every two years. FINTRAC doesn't charge a fee, and it says the majority of complete applications are processed within three months.

Outsourced or in-house compliance officer?

Every business FINTRAC regulates needs a compliance officer. You can appoint the owner or a manager, hire someone, or outsource the role, and FINTRAC's expectations are the same in each case.

Compliance programs

Policies, procedures and risk assessments written for the way your business actually operates.

Businesses regulated by FINTRAC must have a compliance program. It has to work in practice, not only on paper.

A compliance program generally brings together a compliance officer, written policies and procedures, a risk assessment, an ongoing training program, and a regular review of how well it works.

We build or rebuild the parts you need, around your products, your customers and the way your team works.

Who it’s for

  • Newly registered businesses

    Putting a program in place for the first time.

  • Growing businesses

    New products, new markets or more volume than the program was written for.

  • Businesses after a review or examination

    Closing the gaps that were found.

What we do

  • Policies and procedures

    Written for your business, in language your team can follow.

  • Risk assessment

    Your products, customers, channels and geography, assessed and documented.

  • Compliance officer support

    A second pair of hands for the person responsible.

  • Training program

    A plan for ongoing training that fits your staff and your risks.

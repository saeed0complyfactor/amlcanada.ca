How we work

Every engagement starts with a conversation and a written scope.

Compliance work goes better when you know what is happening and why.

We keep engagements simple: a conversation, a written scope, the work itself, and support afterwards. Each step is set out below.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We start with a conversation about your business, your timeline and what is required of you. You leave it with a clear next step.

    • Your business model and the services you offer
    • Your deadlines, such as a launch or an examination
    • What you already have in place
  2. 2

    Scope

    We write down what we will do, what we need from you, and when. Nothing starts until you have agreed it.

    • The work, in plain language
    • What we need from you, and when
    • Timeline and milestones
  3. 3

    Delivery

    We do the work with you, not around you, and explain the decisions along the way so your team can stand behind them.

    • Regular check-ins
    • Drafts you can comment on
    • Decisions explained, not just made
  4. 4

    Ongoing support

    Your obligations change as your business does. We are there for the next product, the next market or the next review.

    • Updates when your business changes
    • Help with questions from FINTRAC
    • Your next effectiveness review

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how AML Canada collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information through amlcanada.ca (the “Website”), our consultation requests, and our enquiry and scoping process. It is written to meet Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Quebec’s Law 25, and Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. Who is responsible

1.1 The organization accountable for your personal information is ComplyFactor Limited, an Ontario corporation (Ontario Corporation No. 1001501600), operating as AML Canada. Registered office: 1025 King Street East, Suite 107 #1596, Cambridge, Ontario N3H 3P5. Principal office: 320 Matheson Boulevard West, Suite 211, Mississauga, Ontario L5R 3R1, Canada.

1.2 Our Privacy Officer is responsible for this policy and for our compliance with privacy law. Contact: Privacy Officer, privacy@amlcanada.ca.

2. What we collect and why

2.1 Information about other people. If you give us information about another person (for example a director or beneficial owner), you must make sure you are entitled to share it and that they know about this policy.

2.2 Information we don’t want. Please do not send us sensitive personal information (such as health information) or suspicious transaction report content through the Website or our enquiry forms.

2.3 Public sources. We may obtain information from public registers (such as FINTRAC’s MSB registry and corporate registries), professional networking sites and adverse-media sources. We use it to verify prospective clients and to meet our own due diligence obligations.

2.4 Automated decisions. We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing.

3. Who we share it with

3.1 We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for third parties’ own marketing.

3.2 We share personal information only with:

  • Our team and affiliates: ComplyFactor Limited, its affiliates, and our employees and contractors, on a need-to-know basis and under confidentiality obligations.
  • Service providers acting for us: website hosting (GitHub Pages), form delivery (Web3Forms), analytics (Cloudflare and, with your consent, Google), and email and productivity tools. Each is bound to use the information only to provide its service to us.
  • Professional advisers: lawyers, accountants, auditors and insurers, under duties of confidentiality.
  • Authorities: regulators, law enforcement, courts and government bodies, where the law requires or permits it.
  • Corporate transactions: a buyer, investor or successor in a merger, sale or reorganization, under confidentiality and only as needed.
  • Others with your consent or at your direction.

4. Where your information is processed

4.1 We operate from Canada. Our service providers store and process information mainly in Canada, the United States and the European Union, so your information may be processed outside your province or country and may be accessible to courts and authorities there under local law.

4.2 Before transferring information outside Quebec, we assess whether it will be adequately protected, as Law 25 requires. You can ask for more detail by contacting our Privacy Officer.

5. How long we keep it

InformationRetention
Enquiries and scoping information that don’t lead to an engagement2 years after last contact

5.1 We may keep information longer where needed to meet a legal obligation or to establish or defend a legal claim. At the end of the retention period, we securely delete or anonymize it.

6. Security

6.1 We protect personal information with safeguards suited to its sensitivity, including encryption in transit, access controls with multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access, confidentiality agreements and staff training. No system is completely secure, and you share information at your own risk.

6.2 If a breach of security safeguards creates a real risk of significant harm (Canada) or a risk of serious injury (Quebec), we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

7.1 The Website does not need cookies to work. Our visitor analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) does not use cookies. We use Google Analytics cookies only if you accept them in our cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the footer, or by blocking cookies in your browser.

8. Your rights

8.1 Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • ask us to delete it, or (in Quebec) to de-index it or stop disseminating it;
  • object to processing for direct marketing;
  • be informed about automated decisions and have a person review them.

8.2 To make a request, email privacy@amlcanada.ca. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within 30 days and tell you if we need longer, as the law allows. We may decline a request where the law permits, for example where disclosure would reveal another person’s information, confidential commercial information, or information relating to a legal or regulatory matter.

8.3 If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec. We’d appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

9. Commercial electronic messages

9.1 We send commercial electronic messages only with your consent: express consent, or implied consent where CASL allows it (within 6 months of an enquiry you made, or 2 years of a purchase or engagement). Every message identifies us and includes a working unsubscribe link, and we action unsubscribes within 10 business days.

10. Children

10.1 Our services are for businesses and professionals. They are not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

11.1 We may update this policy. We will post the new version with its effective date and, where a change is material, tell you by email if we hold your address.

Training

AML training for front-line staff and compliance teams, built around situations they will recognize.

An ongoing compliance training program is one of the required parts of a compliance program.

Training works when staff recognize the situation in front of them and know what to do next. We build training around real scenarios for your business.

We train front-line staff and compliance teams, and help you keep the records that show training happened.

Who it’s for

  • Front-line staff

    The people who meet customers and process transactions.

  • Compliance teams

    Analysts and compliance officers who need depth.

  • Senior management

    Leaders responsible for the program’s oversight.

What we do

  • Scenario-based training

    Built around situations your staff will recognize.

  • Training for each role

    Front line, compliance and management.

  • Training records

    Evidence of who was trained, on what, and when.

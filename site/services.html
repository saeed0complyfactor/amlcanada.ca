Services

Registration, compliance programs, reviews and training for businesses regulated by FINTRAC.

From your first registration to your next examination, we help you meet your obligations and show that you have.

Canadian addressA Canadian business address for companies setting up in Canada.MSB registrationRegistration with FINTRAC as a money services business, including foreign MSBs and virtual currency dealers.Compliance programsPolicies and procedures, risk assessment and compliance officer support.Effectiveness reviewsA structured review of your program, and preparation for a FINTRAC examination.TrainingScenario-based AML training for front-line staff and compliance teams.

How we work

  1. Consultation

    A conversation about your business, your timeline and what is required of you.

  2. Scope

    A written scope of the work, so you know what you are getting before anything starts.

  3. Delivery

    We do the work with you and explain the decisions along the way.

  4. Ongoing support

    Help as your business, your products or your obligations change.

Effectiveness reviews

Find the gaps in your compliance program before an examiner does.

Reporting entities must review the effectiveness of their compliance program every two years.

An effectiveness review looks at whether your policies, risk assessment and training work in practice, and whether what your team does matches what your documents say.

We also help you prepare for a FINTRAC examination: what to expect, what to have ready, and what to fix first.

Who it’s for

  • Reporting entities due a review

    Including businesses whose last review is approaching two years.

  • Businesses expecting an examination

    Getting ready before FINTRAC arrives.

  • New compliance officers

    Taking stock of a program they have inherited.

What we do

  • A scoped review

    Agreed in writing before the work starts.

  • Testing in practice

    Checking that what happens matches what is written.

  • Written findings

    Clear findings and recommendations, in order of priority.

  • Examination preparation

    Help getting your records and your team ready.

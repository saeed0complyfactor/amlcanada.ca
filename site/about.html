About AML Canada

AML and anti-terrorist financing compliance for businesses regulated by FINTRAC.

AML Canada helps businesses register with FINTRAC, build compliance programs that work, prepare for examinations and train their people.

FINTRAC is Canada’s financial intelligence unit. The businesses it regulates carry real obligations, and most of them are busy running everything else.

We exist to make those obligations clear and manageable, from the first registration to the next review.

What we believe

  • Clear answers

    People come to us under pressure. We tell you plainly what applies to you and what happens next.

  • Specific, not generic

    Programs written for your business, not templates with your name on them.

  • Accuracy first

    We check every regulatory statement before we rely on it.

  • One next step

    Every conversation ends with a clear next step.
